# Brand Showcase — Instagram

## Goal

Build a recognizable brand identity on Instagram that drives organic reach, saves, shares, and profile visits — not just likes.

## Why Instagram Matters for Brands

- **2B+ monthly users** with high purchase intent in discovery feeds
- **Reels algorithm** favors new accounts with strong hooks (3-second rule)
- **Visual-first platform** — brand aesthetics (colors, fonts, tone) directly impact follow rate
- **Shopping integration** — Instagram Shop + product tags bridge brand → purchase

## Best Approach to Go Viral

### 1. Define Your Brand DNA First

Before posting, lock in from Business DNA → Overview:

- **Consistent color palette** (use all 5 swatches across posts)
- **One primary font** for overlays and Stories
- **Brand tone tags** (e.g., Premium, Calm) — every caption should match
- **Tagline** under 8 words for bio and Reels text overlays

### 2. Content Pillars (70/20/10 Rule)

| Pillar | % | Examples |
|--------|---|----------|
| Value / Education | 70% | Tips, behind-the-brand, UGC reposts |
| Entertainment / Trend | 20% | Reels using trending audio, memes in brand voice |
| Promotion | 10% | Product launches, sales, Shop links |

### 3. Reels Strategy (Highest Viral Potential)

**Hook formula (first 3 seconds):**
- Pattern interrupt: unexpected visual or statement
- Text overlay: "Stop scrolling if you [pain point]"
- Face + product within frame 1

**Specs:**
- 9:16 vertical, 15–30 seconds
- Captions burned in (85% watch muted)
- Post 4–7 Reels/week for algorithm favor

### 4. Carousel Posts for Brand Story

- Slide 1: Bold headline + brand logo
- Slides 2–4: Brand values, founder story, social proof
- Final slide: CTA ("Follow for more" / "Link in bio")
- 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio, 5–8 slides optimal

### 5. Hashtag & Discovery

- **3 tiers:** 3 broad (500K+), 5 medium (50K–500K), 7 niche (<50K)
- Place in first comment, not caption (cleaner look)
- Create a **branded hashtag** (#YourBrandName) and encourage UGC

### 6. Collaboration & UGC

- Micro-influencers (10K–100K) in your niche — 3–5x ROI vs macro
- Repost customer content with permission (builds trust)
- "Get featured" CTA in bio drives submissions

### 7. Posting Cadence

| Format | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|
| Reels | 4–7/week |
| Carousels | 2–3/week |
| Stories | Daily (3–7 frames) |
| Lives | 1–2/month |

### 8. Metrics That Signal Virality

- **Save rate** > like rate (algorithm boost)
- **Share rate** — strongest viral signal
- **Profile visits** from Reels — brand discovery
- **Reach vs. followers** ratio > 3x = viral content

## Brand Asset Checklist (Use Photoshoot Templates)

- [ ] Studio shot (profile grid consistency)
- [ ] Lifestyle / human (Reels B-roll)
- [ ] Flatlay (carousel slide 1)
- [ ] Seasonal / event (trend-jacking)

## Integration with Advertisement AI Tool

1. Complete **Brand Overview** (logo, colors, fonts, values)
2. Upload brand assets in **Assets**
3. Select **Instagram Story Promo**, **Carousel**, or **Reels Hook** campaign templates
4. Generate on-brand creatives using your Business DNA palette

## Common Mistakes

- Inconsistent visuals across posts (breaks brand recognition)
- Only promotional content (algorithm penalizes)
- Ignoring Stories (highest engagement surface)
- No link in bio strategy (use Linktree or native Shop)
