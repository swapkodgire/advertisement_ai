# Brand Showcase — Facebook

## Goal

Build a loyal brand community on Facebook through Groups, Reels, and Meta Ads — driving repeat engagement and word-of-mouth sharing.

## Why Facebook Matters for Brands

- **3B+ Meta ecosystem users** (Facebook + Instagram + WhatsApp)
- **Older, higher-spending demographics** (25–54) for many e-commerce categories
- **Facebook Groups** create sticky brand communities
- **Meta Advantage+** campaigns automate creative testing at scale

## Best Approach to Go Viral

### 1. Facebook Page Foundation

From Business DNA, sync:
- Logo, cover photo (820×312), about section with tagline
- Business hours, location, phone, website CTA
- Shop tab connected to catalog
- **Username** matching brand name (@yourbrand)

### 2. Content Mix for Organic Reach

| Type | Purpose | Frequency |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Native video | Highest reach | 3–5/week |
| Photo albums | Product launches | 1–2/week |
| Polls / questions | Engagement bait (authentic) | 2/week |
| Live shopping | Conversion events | 1–2/month |
| Group posts | Community depth | Daily |

**Key insight:** Facebook deprioritizes links in posts. Put links in first comment; lead with native video/image.

### 3. Facebook Reels

- Repurpose Instagram Reels (9:16) — Meta cross-surfaces
- Add **Facebook-specific captions** (slightly longer, conversational)
- Use Reels tab on Page for discovery beyond followers

### 4. Facebook Groups Strategy

Create or nurture a **brand community group:**
- Name: "[Brand Name] Insiders" or "[Category] by [Brand]"
- Exclusive drops, early access, UGC showcases
- Groups generate **notification-level engagement** Pages cannot match
- Viral loop: members invite friends → group growth → brand advocacy

### 5. Meta Ads — Brand Awareness

**Campaign objectives:**
- **Awareness:** Reach + Brand Awareness (CPM optimization)
- **Engagement:** Video views, Page likes, event responses
- **Advantage+ Shopping:** Full funnel with brand assets

**Creative specs:**
- Single image: 1:1 or 4:5, text <20% of image (guideline)
- Video: 15s optimal, square or vertical, captions required
- Carousel: 3–5 cards telling brand story → product

Use **Facebook Single Image Ad**, **Carousel Ad**, and **Video Ad** campaign templates.

### 6. Social Proof & Testimonials

From Business Details → Testimonials:
- Turn quotes into **testimonial graphic posts**
- Use in ad creative (with permission)
- Video testimonials outperform static 3:1

### 7. Viral Mechanics

- **Giveaways:** Tag 2 friends + follow (compliant with Meta rules)
- **Challenges:** UGC hashtag campaigns
- **Milestone posts:** "10K customers — thank you" (shareable)
- **Behind-the-scenes:** Factory, packaging, team — humanizes brand

### 8. Retargeting Brand Story

Custom audiences:
- Video viewers (25%, 50%, 95%)
- Page engagers (365 days)
- Website visitors

Show **brand story ads** before product ads — warms cold audiences.

## Brand Asset Checklist

- [ ] Cover photo + profile logo
- [ ] 5+ video assets (15s, 30s, 60s)
- [ ] Testimonial graphics
- [ ] Carousel brand story (5 cards)
- [ ] Group welcome post + rules

## Metrics

- **Share rate** on video posts
- **Group member growth** and active members
- **Ad recall lift** (Brand Awareness campaigns)
- **Cost per Page engagement**

## Integration with Advertisement AI Tool

1. Add **Facebook** social link in Business Details
2. Import testimonials for ad creative
3. Use **Human Lifestyle** and **Event/Contextual** photoshoot templates
4. Generate **Facebook Carousel** and **Video Ad** campaigns

## Common Mistakes

- Only posting product links (reach penalty)
- Ignoring Groups (missing highest-engagement surface)
- No video content (Facebook is video-first in 2026)
- Inconsistent posting (algorithm decay after 2 weeks inactive)
- Boosting every post instead of strategic paid campaigns
