# Brand Showcase — Google

## Goal

Establish brand authority in Google Search, Shopping, YouTube, and Display so customers find and trust your brand at every stage of intent.

## Why Google Matters for Brands

- **High purchase intent** — users are actively searching
- **Performance Max (PMax)** uses brand assets across Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Maps
- **Google Business Profile** drives local brand discovery
- **AI Overviews & SGE** increasingly cite structured brand data

## Best Approach to Go Viral (Search & Discovery)

### 1. Brand Entity Optimization

Google treats brands as **entities**. Strengthen yours:

- **Consistent NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) across website, GBP, social
- **Organization schema** on homepage with logo, sameAs social links
- **Knowledge Panel triggers:** Wikipedia mention, press coverage, structured data

From Business DNA → Business Details, ensure:
- Location, phone, hours are identical everywhere
- All social links populated (feeds `sameAs` in schema)
- Keywords reflect how people search your category

### 2. Google Business Profile (GBP)

- Complete every field: logo, cover, products, services, Q&A
- Post **weekly updates** (offers, events, new products) — boosts local pack
- Collect and respond to **reviews** (quality + velocity matter)
- Add **brand photos** from Photoshoot (studio + lifestyle)

### 3. Performance Max Brand Campaigns

**Asset groups need:**
- 15 headlines (30 char) — include brand name in 5+
- 4 long headlines (90 char)
- 5 descriptions (90 char)
- Logo: 1:1 and 4:1 variants
- Images: min 1200×628 (landscape), 1200×1200 (square)
- Video: 10s+ horizontal and vertical

**Viral loop via PMax:** Strong assets → more impressions → more brand searches → lower CPA over time.

### 4. YouTube Brand Channel

- **Shorts** (9:16, <60s) — fastest growth for new brands
- Brand intro video (30s) for PMax and website
- Product demos with consistent brand intro/outro
- Optimize titles: `[Brand Name] + [keyword]` for search

### 5. SEO Content for Brand Terms

Target:
- `[Brand name] reviews`
- `[Brand name] vs [competitor]`
- `Best [category] brands`
- `[Brand name] discount code`

Publish 2–4 authoritative blog posts/month with internal links to product pages.

### 6. Google Shopping Brand Presence

- Submit **brand name** in Merchant Center
- Use **lifestyle + white background** images (Amazon White BG template)
- Enable **free listings** + paid Shopping simultaneously

### 7. AI Overviews / SGE Readiness

- FAQ schema on brand FAQ page
- Clear "About Us" with E-E-A-T signals (founder, mission, credentials)
- Structured product data feeds AI shopping answers

- **Impression share** on brand campaigns
- **GBP views & direction requests**
- **YouTube Shorts views** from non-subscribers

## Integration with Advertisement AI Tool

1. Fill **Business Details** (location, keywords, social links)
2. Use **Google PMax** and **Google Search Ad Copy** campaign templates
3. Generate **Pure White Background** + **Outdoor Lifestyle** photoshoot assets for Merchant Center

## Common Mistakes

- Inconsistent business name spelling across platforms
- Missing structured data
- Only running non-brand Search campaigns (competitors capture your brand traffic)
- Low-quality or low-resolution PMax images (limited ad serving)
