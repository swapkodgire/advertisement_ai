# Product Showcase — Instagram

## Goal

Turn individual catalog products into scroll-stopping Instagram content that drives saves, shares, Shop clicks, and purchases.

## Product vs. Brand on Instagram

| Brand Showcase | Product Showcase |
|----------------|------------------|
| Logo, values, story | Single SKU focus |
| Follow-worthy | Buy-worthy |
| Consistent grid aesthetic | Feature/benefit hooks |
| Community building | Conversion optimization |

## Best Approach for Product Virality

### 1. Start with a Strong Raw Photo

In Catalog → Add from scratch:
- Upload a **clean, well-lit raw product photo**
- Neutral background preferred (AI templates work best)
- Multiple angles if available (carousel advantage)

### 2. Photoshoot Template Selection (Per Product)

| Template | Best For | Format |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Studio Shot | Grid consistency, Shop thumbnail | 1:1 |
| Ingredient/Lifestyle | Food, beauty, wellness | 4:5 |
| Human Lifestyle | Fashion, accessories, fitness | 4:5 |
| Action Shot | Beverages, skincare application | 9:16 Reels |
| Flatlay | Fashion, accessories collections | 1:1 |
| Model Try On | Apparel | 4:5 |

Select up to **4 templates** per product photoshoot session.

### 3. Reels Product Hooks (Viral Formula)

**Structure (15–30s):**
1. **Hook (0–3s):** Problem or bold claim — "This $29 pouch replaced my entire travel kit"
2. **Demo (3–15s):** Product in use, unboxing, before/after
3. **Proof (15–22s):** Review overlay, star rating, "10K sold"
4. **CTA (22–30s):** "Link in bio" / "Shop now" + product tag

**Specs:** 9:16, captions burned in, trending audio (check commercial use rights)

### 4. Carousel Product Posts

- Slide 1: Hero lifestyle shot (stop the scroll)
- Slide 2: Problem statement (text overlay)
- Slide 3–4: Features with icons
- Slide 5: Social proof / UGC
- Slide 6: CTA + price

Carousels get **higher save rates** — strong viral signal for products.

### 5. Instagram Shop Optimization

- Product title: `[Brand] + [Product Name] + [Key Attribute]`
- Description: First 2 lines = hook (visible without "more")
- Tag products in **every** Reels and carousel
- Enable checkout if eligible (reduces friction)

### 6. UGC & Unboxing Loop

- Send product to 10–20 micro-creators
- Provide **shot list** using your Photoshoot templates as reference
- Repost with `#YourBrand` — product-specific UGC outperforms studio shots 2:1 for trust

### 7. Seasonal Product Push

Use **Event/Contextual** template:
- Holiday gift guides
- Summer/outdoor variants
- Trend-jack within 48 hours of trending topic

## Campaign Templates to Use

- **Instagram Story Promo** — flash sales, 24h urgency
- **Instagram Carousel** — feature breakdown
- **Reels Hook Campaign** — viral discovery

## Metrics

- **Product tag clicks** per post
- **Save-to-reach ratio** (target >2%)
- **Shop conversion rate**
- **Reels reach from non-followers**

## Integration with Advertisement AI Tool

1. Add product in **Catalog** (URL or scratch with raw photo)
2. Click **Create Photoshoot** → select 4 templates
3. Click **Create Campaign** → pick Instagram templates
4. Apply Business DNA colors/fonts to generated overlays

## Common Mistakes

- Only studio shots (no lifestyle = low engagement)
- No product tags in Reels
- Generic captions without benefit hooks
- Same creative for feed and Stories (resize + adapt)
- Ignoring 4:5 ratio (more feed real estate than 1:1)
