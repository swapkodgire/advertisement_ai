# Product Showcase — Google

## Goal

Maximize product visibility in Google Shopping, Search ads, and AI-powered shopping results with compliant, high-converting product assets.

## Product Discovery on Google

- **Shopping tab** — visual product search
- **Performance Max** — product-led asset groups
- **Search RSAs** — product-specific ad copy
- **AI Overviews** — product recommendations with images and prices
- **Free listings** — organic Shopping presence

## Best Approach for Product Virality

### 1. Merchant Center Feed Excellence

Every catalog item needs:

| Field | Best Practice |
|-------|---------------|
| title | `[Brand] [Product] - [Color/Size/Key Feature]` (150 char max) |
| description | 500–5000 chars, bullet benefits, materials, use cases |
| image_link | Min 800×800, white or lifestyle |
| additional_image_link | Up to 10 — use all Photoshoot variants |
| brand | Exact match to Business DNA name |
| gtin/mpn | Required for most categories |
| google_product_category | Accurate taxonomy |

### 2. Image Strategy (Photoshoot Templates)

**Required variants per SKU:**

| Template | Use Case |
|----------|----------|
| Pure White Background | Primary listing image (Amazon/Google compliant) |
| Outdoor Lifestyle | PMax, Display, AI shopping cards |
| Indoor Lifestyle | Home/category pages, remarketing |
| Studio Shot | Backup primary if white BG unavailable |

**Rules:**
- No watermarks, promotional text, or borders on primary image
- Product fills 75–90% of frame
- Lifestyle images as `additional_image_link`

### 3. Performance Max Product Groups

Structure campaigns:
- **One asset group per hero product** (top 20% SKUs)
- **Category asset groups** for long tail
- Feed labels for: `bestseller`, `new`, `margin_high`

Include in each group:
- 5+ unique headlines mentioning product name
- 3+ descriptions with USP
- All image aspect ratios (1.91:1, 1:1, 4:5)
- 15s product video if available

Use **Google Performance Max** and **Google Shopping Feed** campaign templates.

### 4. Product Listing Ads (PLA) Optimization

- **Custom labels:** margin tier, season, stock level
- **Promotions:** sale_price + sale_price_effective_date in feed
- **Local inventory ads** if physical retail exists

### 5. SEO Product Pages

Each product page needs:
- Unique title tag: `[Product] | [Brand]`
- Product schema (Offer, AggregateRating, Review)
- High-res images with descriptive alt text
- FAQ section (feeds AI Overviews)

Use **Google Search Ad Copy** template for RSA headline/description variants.

### 6. AI Shopping Readiness

For Gemini / AI Overviews product cards:
- Structured `Product` JSON-LD with price, availability, image
- Clear comparison attributes (size, material, warranty)
- Sync Merchant Center ↔ website pricing (discrepancies hurt trust scores)

Use **AI Search Product Card** campaign template.

### 7. YouTube Shopping

- Product demo Shorts (9:16, link to product page)
- Link products in YouTube Studio
- Shorts feed Shopping suggestions for engaged viewers

## Metrics

- **Click-through rate** on Shopping ads (benchmark 0.5–1.5%)
- **Conversion rate** by product
- **Impression share** on top SKUs
- **Free listing clicks** vs. paid

## Integration with Advertisement AI Tool

1. **Catalog → Add from URL** to import product data
2. **Photoshoot:** Pure White BG + Outdoor + Indoor templates
3. **Campaign:** Google Shopping Feed + PMax + AI Product Card
4. Map Business DNA keywords to product titles

## Common Mistakes

- Single image per SKU (misses PMax placement opportunities)
- Promotional text on primary white-bg image (feed disapproval)
- Generic titles without brand + attributes
- Price mismatch between feed and landing page
- No product schema on site
