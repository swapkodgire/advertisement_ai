# Product Showcase — Facebook

## Goal

Drive product sales through Facebook Shop, dynamic product ads, and viral UGC-style creative that gets shared within friend networks.

## Product Advertising on Meta

- **Facebook/Instagram Shop** — unified catalog
- **Advantage+ Catalog Ads** — automated product retargeting
- **Dynamic Ads (DPA)** — show exact products users viewed
- **Collection Ads** — instant storefront experience
- **Reels** — product discovery beyond followers

## Best Approach for Product Virality

### 1. Catalog Setup

Sync Catalog items from Business DNA → Catalog:

| Field | Facebook Requirement |
|-------|---------------------|
| id | Unique SKU |
| title | 65 char recommended |
| description | 9999 char max, front-load benefits |
| availability | in stock / out of stock |
| condition | new |
| price | With currency |
| link | Product landing page |
| image_link | Min 500×500, prefer 1080×1080 |
| brand | From Business DNA |

Use **Add from URL** to import, then enrich with Photoshoot images.

### 2. Product Creative Strategy

**Top-performing formats:**

| Format | Template | Spec |
|--------|----------|------|
| Single Image Ad | Facebook Single Image Ad | 1:1 or 4:5 |
| Carousel | Facebook Carousel Ad | 1:1, 2–10 cards |
| Video | Facebook Video Ad | 1:1 or 4:5, 15s |
| Collection | Hero + product grid | 1200×628 hero |

**Creative rules:**
- Product visible in first frame (video)
- Price or offer in overlay (where policy allows)
- Lifestyle > white background for cold audiences
- White background / studio for retargeting (recognition)

### 3. Photoshoot Templates for Facebook Products

| Audience | Template |
|----------|----------|
| Cold (prospecting) | Human Lifestyle, Action Shot, Event/Contextual |
| Warm (retargeting) | Studio Shot, Pure White Background |
| Fashion | Model Try On, Flatlay |
| Beauty | In Use, Floating with Bubbles |
| Home | Bedroom Lifestyle, Home Studio |

Select 4 templates per product; test winners in Ads Manager.

### 4. Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC)

- Upload full catalog
- Provide 10+ creative variants per hero SKU
- Let Meta optimize creative × audience × placement
- Include **Instagram + Facebook + Audience Network**

Minimum creative mix per product:
- 2 images (lifestyle + studio)
- 1 video (15s demo)
- 1 carousel (features)

### 5. Dynamic Product Ads (Retargeting)

Audiences:
- Viewed product (7 days)
- Added to cart (14 days)
- Purchased (exclude, upsell cross-sells)

Creative: Studio/product-on-white for instant recognition + "Still thinking about this?" copy

### 6. Viral Product Mechanics on Facebook

- **Tag-a-friend giveaways** for hero products
- **Unboxing videos** from real customers (UGC ads perform 4x vs. studio)
- **Limited drops** announced in Groups first (exclusivity drives shares)
- **Before/after** for beauty, home, fitness products

### 7. Social Proof in Product Ads

From Business Details → Testimonials:
- Overlay quotes on product images
- "4.8★ from 200+ reviews" in primary text
- Video testimonial ads (15s)

### 8. Copy Framework

**Primary text (125 char visible):**
`[Hook/Problem]. [Product] [key benefit]. [Social proof]. [CTA]`

**Headline (40 char):**
`[Product Name] — [Offer/USP]`

**Description (30 char):**
`Free shipping · 30-day returns`

Use **Facebook Single Image Ad** and **Cross-Platform Launch Kit** templates.

## Metrics

- **ROAS** by product (target 3x+ for prospecting)
- **CTR** on dynamic ads (benchmark 1–2%)
- **Add-to-cart rate** from Collection ads
- **Share rate** on UGC video ads

## Integration with Advertisement AI Tool

1. Add products to **Catalog** with raw photos
2. **Create Photoshoot** → Human Lifestyle + Studio + category templates
3. **Create Campaign** → Facebook Single Image, Carousel, Video
4. Export images to Meta Commerce Manager / Ads Manager

## Common Mistakes

- Catalog not synced (broken dynamic ads)
- Only one image per product (creative fatigue in 3–5 days)
- No video assets (Meta strongly favors video)
- Landing page mismatch (ad product ≠ page product)
- Ignoring Advantage+ (manual targeting underperforms in 2026)
- Promotional overlay >20% on image (delivery penalty)
